The actions of library-rack's DUT, implemented in Go in main.go (dutRack.Run).
The contract names this file, and both racks hash it: a change to what an
action does is a change to this file, and so to the contract.

enter_active   Nothing: the simulated DUT is always active while it runs.
leave_active   Nothing.
reset          Sends the DUT a reset, and ends once it is running again, or
               fails a second after.
